Striking words inside an Experience

Prompt 39a: “Strikeout three nouns from the Memories in your Diary, starting from the oldest entry.” The words have to stay visible and negated — that is what striking means on paper — so this is not the same operation as editing them away.

1 — Reading

    The Experience rows are the app's own. Struck words carry the same line-through and dimming the app already uses for a struck-out Trait or a lost Memory — no new visual language.

    2 — Striking

      Turn the mode on and every word becomes its own tap target — tap to strike it, tap again to restore it. No text cursor, no selection handles, no keyboard: on a phone, selecting three exact words with a caret is miserable, and this is three taps. The sentence stays readable the whole time, so you are choosing words in context rather than in an edit field.

      3 — What gets stored
      experience.text

      Still one plain string. The markers are what the tapping writes, and the player never types them — so updateMemoryExperience, saving, and export all keep working unchanged, and an Experience pasted into any other app still reads correctly. The cost is that everything which displays an Experience has to render the markers rather than print them raw.
